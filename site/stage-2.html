Building Trust

Install a shared language — your marker word, their name, your touch rules and reading their signals.

Start when your dog is eating, sleeping and settling — usually after the first three to five days; keep sessions optional. Now that meals, sleep and toilets are predictable, your dog can start learning a language with you. This language has just four words to start: the marker that says 'reward coming', their name that says 'look at me', your palm that says 'come with me', and consent that says 'you decide about touch'. All of it is taught with tiny food payments while your dog is calm, indoors, in boring rooms — and all of it makes everything later easier.

Every exercise in this stage is mastered.
