Every meal becomes a good-news game

5–10 minutes per meal
The goal

Split every meal into sniffing, hand-feeding and puzzle parts, so you become the best thing in their day.

Why this matters

Food is the fastest trust-builder you have. Hand-feeding and scatter-feeding teach your dog that good things happen near this human — not just that people take things away. Sniffing out food also settles an anxious body, and your dog learns that learning from you pays.

What you need
  • Their daily food ration
  • A mat or grass patch
  • A stuffed Kong or puzzle toy
  • A quiet corner

Steps

Common mistakes

  • Never hover, reach or lean over their food — you're the source of good things, not a guard.
  • Don't skip meals to 'build appetite' — to a stressed dog, a skipped meal reads as scarcity. If your dog still isn't eating after a day, ask the vet before changing anything.
  • Don't let kids crawl near feeding time — ever, in every week and every stage of this course.

Mastery check

These should be true in a normal session, on a normal day.

If your dog is struggling

They won't eat at all.

Normal for the first days — stress kills appetite. Toss food from a distance and back off; if they truly eat nothing for 24+ hours, call the vet.

They wolf food and seem edgy when I pass by.

Scatter from further away NOW; stand beyond arm's reach, and let approach shrink over weeks — see the bowl exercise in Stage 5 before ever going closer.
