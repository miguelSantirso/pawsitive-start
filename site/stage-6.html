Growing Together

Prove recall outdoors, add joy with tricks, polish guest and car manners, and set your next goals.

By now your dog knows the language, walks with a slack leash, rests alone and gives you consent. This stage is where trust becomes joy: an outdoor recall you can actually count on, two tricks that make guests smile, calm guest arrivals and car rides, and a graduation plan that keeps you training into year one. It's also the stage where you decide together what 'a good life for this particular dog' looks like.

Every exercise in this stage is mastered.
