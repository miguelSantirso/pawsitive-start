Work through the stages in order. Each one makes the next easier.

  1. 01 Welcome HomeDays 1–3 · 4 exercisesSafety, sleep and routine first. Nothing is asked of your dog yet except feeling okay.
  2. 02 Building TrustUsually days 3–14 · 6 exercisesInstall a shared language — your marker word, their name, your touch rules and reading their signals.
  3. 03 First WordsWeeks 2–5 · 6 exercisesSay what you want: sit, down, settle, come, leave it, drop it — all taught indoors where it's easy.
  4. 04 Out in the WorldWeeks 4–10 · 6 exercisesTake the polished indoor skills outside in slices: gear, doors, quiet streets, sniff walks and calm passing.
  5. 05 Calm & ConfidenceWeeks 6–12 · 5 exercisesThe keeper-skills: resting alone, quiet on cue, being handled, and food safety.
  6. 06 Growing TogetherMonths 3–6 · 6 exercisesProve recall outdoors, add joy with tricks, polish guest and car manners, and set your next goals.

How this works

01

Start at Stage 1 — even if you've had dogs before

Shelter dogs need the decompression weeks first: safe space, sleep, routine. Skipping decompression can make a nervous dog more reactive; start with safety and routine.

02

One exercise at a time, a few minutes

Do today's focus exercise, tick the steps as you go, and check the Mastery criteria. When it's solid, mark it mastered and celebrate — small wins stack.

03

Stuck? Every exercise has a safety net

Troubleshooting tips under each exercise, the Golden Rules page for principles, and gentle 'consistent, not perfect' progress. Your streak never gets punished.
